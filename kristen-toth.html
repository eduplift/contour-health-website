About Kristen

Kristen bridges the gap between conventional clinical care and proactive health optimization. After beginning her career in the ER and successfully navigating her own complex health challenges with Hashimoto's and mold exposure, Kristen shifted her focus entirely to functional medicine — recognizing it as the premier path to long-term vitality.

At Contour, Kristen looks beyond the traditional model of mere symptom management. Instead, she utilizes a root-cause approach, analyzing your complete physiology — from advanced diagnostics and environmental factors to nervous system regulation. She works as a dedicated partner, building personalized plans grounded in precision functional medicine to help you achieve peak performance and lasting health.

Functional medicine

Functional medicine with Kristen Toth, PA-C

Functional medicine is a patient-centered, science-based framework that focuses on the root causes of health concerns rather than managing symptoms in isolation. Kristen shifted her focus entirely to functional medicine after beginning her career in the ER and navigating her own health challenges with Hashimoto's and mold exposure.

At Contour Health, a functional medicine evaluation draws on a detailed health history and, where appropriate, advanced laboratory testing, which can include comprehensive metabolic panels, microbiome analysis, nutritional assessments, and hormonal evaluations. Membership includes a 60 to 90 minute functional evaluation, and your plan is personalized to you.

Read more in What Is Functional Medicine? or see what is included on the membership and pricing page.

Women's health

Women's health: a root-cause starting point

Many women look for answers about concerns such as perimenopause and menopause, PCOS, fatigue, hair loss, thyroid health, and weight. At Contour Health, the starting point for any of these is the same: a detailed health history, a root-cause approach, and a review of laboratory results, including hormonal evaluations when appropriate.

Thyroid health is personal for Kristen. Her own experience with Hashimoto's is part of what led her to functional medicine. For weight, Contour Health offers a metabolic weight loss plan with clinician oversight and a functional registered dietitian.

This page does not describe a specific treatment for any of these concerns, and nothing here is a promise of any result. Whether Contour Health and Kristen are the right fit for your situation is something we talk through on a complimentary discovery call. Start with the I'm Interested form.

How do I book with Kristen?

What should I expect at a first visit?

After the discovery call, Contour Health schedules an initial consultation, a 60 to 90 minute deep dive into your current health and history, training, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle, biomarkers and any prior testing, and your short- and long-term goals. From there your clinician builds a personalized plan.
